Location QR Code (Google Maps)

Scanning opens a map at your exact coordinates, so visitors stop hunting for the entrance.

Last updated: October 2026 · Free · no account · no watermark

What it does: stores a latitude and longitude. Scanning opens the phone's default map app at that point, usually with a pin you can tap for directions.

A location QR code turns “we're behind the blue door on the left” into a pin on a map. It is the fastest way to fix the last fifty metres of a journey, which is where most visitors actually get lost.

The code stores coordinates rather than a link, so it works with whatever map app the person already uses. You can pick the point by searching for an address or by dragging a marker on the map.

How to create a location QR code

  1. Select the Location type Open Make My QR Code and choose Location.
  2. Find your spot Search for the address, or drag the marker on the map until it sits exactly on the entrance. A driveway or car park entrance is usually the right point.
  3. Check the coordinates The latitude and longitude fields update as you move the pin. Read them back to confirm.
  4. Download the PNG Generate at a large size if it will be printed on signage.
  5. Walk the route Scan it from the street and follow the directions as a first-time visitor would.

Where people use it

  • Storefronts and market stalls
  • Event venues and wedding locations
  • Car parks and delivery drop-off points
  • Trailheads, viewpoints and tourist spots
  • Property listings and open-house signs
  • Warehouse and loading bay entrances

Best practices

  • Pin the entrance, not the centre of the building. Map apps will otherwise route people to the back.
  • If your business already has a Google Business Profile, a link QR code to that listing can be more useful &mdash; it shows reviews and hours too.
  • Be precise about coordinates in rural areas where address lookup is unreliable.
  • Add a one-line instruction such as &ldquo;Scan for directions&rdquo;.

Frequently asked questions

Which map app opens?

The phone's default, which is usually Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. Both accept raw coordinates.

Can I make it open Google Maps specifically?

Coordinates open in whichever app is default. If you need Google Maps regardless of device, use a link QR code pointing at a Google Maps URL instead.

Does it work without an internet connection?

The code itself scans offline, but the map needs data to load tiles and calculate a route.

Can I add a label or business name?

Not reliably. Most map apps show a bare pin. Put the name in the printed text next to the code.
