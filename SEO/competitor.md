## 1. Market Overview

The QR code generator market has matured significantly. Key trends in 2026:

- **Dynamic QR codes are the standard** — users expect editability and scan tracking
- **AI-powered QR art** is emerging as a differentiator (QR-Verse leading this)
- **No-signup static QR generation** is table stakes — every competitor offers it
- **Monetization shift** — free static QR = free; dynamic QR = subscription model
- **Enterprise features** (API, bulk creation, white-label, CRM integrations) drive B2B revenue
- **Mobile apps** becoming important (QR TIGER, QR-Verse have iOS/Android apps)
- **Branded/colored QR codes with logo** are now expected, not premium

### Strengths
- **#1 brand recognition** for "free QR code generator" — millions of users
- **No signup required** to create and download high-quality QR codes
- **Best free design customization** — gradient colors, custom eye shapes, body shapes
- **Vector formats free** — SVG, EPS, PDF available at no cost
- **No expiration, no scan limits** on generated codes

- **No mobile app** — desktop-only web tool
- **No team/collaboration features**
- **Monetization gap** — relies on ads + upselling to "QR Code Generator PRO" (third-party)

### Target Audience
Designers, small businesses, and individuals who need a one-time, permanent QR code with good design options at zero cost.
